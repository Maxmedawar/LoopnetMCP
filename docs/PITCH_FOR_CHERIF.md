# MedawarCRE — The Full Story, In Plain English

**One sentence:** This is your FACTS process turned into a machine — it finds the deal,
checks every number against the real documents, helps control the property before buying,
watches every deadline, and remembers everything — so a person with theory can execute
with the discipline of a 30-year pro.

It speaks normal English. You talk to it like a person: *"Find me NNN retail in San

## STAGE 1 — FINDING DEALS

**On-market (what everyone can see):**
- Searches LoopNet and Crexi live, right now, real listings.
- Scores every deal against YOUR criteria: cap rate, lease years left, tenant quality,
  price per foot.
- Spots the same building listed by two brokers at two prices.
- Watches your saved searches and tells you only about NEW deals that fit.
- Flags stale listings (sat 200+ days, price cut twice) = seller may take a low offer
  or creative terms.

**Off-market (where the real money is):**
- Finds owners in trouble: foreclosures, REO, tax sales, tax delinquency.
- Reads county records: who owns it, how long, what they paid.
- Scores "owner motivation" — divorce, estate, loan coming due, vacant building.
- Finds owners who own 5+ properties (portfolio sellers), owners sitting on one odd
  property far from their others.
- Finds stalled construction projects (permit pulled, then nothing happened for a year).
- Finds sale-leaseback candidates (business owns its own building).
- Writes the first letter/email/call script to that specific owner.

## STAGE 2 — CHECKING THE LOCATION (before you waste a trip)

- **Who's nearby:** pulls every branded business around the site — Starbucks, Chase,
  Chipotle — real data, real distances. Anchors that bring traffic vs. competitors.
- **Traffic:** uses the daily car count (AADT) on that road in the tenant-fit math.
- **The corner itself:** corner or mid-block? How many feet of frontage? Signalized
  intersection? How many ways in and out of the parking lot? Median blocking left turns?
  It scores all of it and tells you which facts are still missing.
- **Zoning:** what is this land allowed to be? It pulls the zoning district live from the
  city (Austin, Phoenix, Denver, Dallas, Atlanta, Vegas, NYC, SF, Seattle — and it knows
  Houston has no zoning at all).
- **Permits:** what's being built nearby (new supply = future competition).
- **Environment:** checks EPA databases around the site — old gas stations, dry cleaners,
  contamination cases, flood zone, wildfire, earthquake risk — before you ever pay for
  a Phase I.
- **Jobs:** watches state layoff notices (WARN) so you know if the area's big employer
  is leaving.

## STAGE 3 — CHECKING THE NUMBERS (the part that saves you from bad deals)

This is the heart of it. **The broker's flyer is a story. The lease is the truth.**

- Upload the offering memo, the rent roll, the T-12, the actual leases.
- It reads them ALL and compares them. When the flyer says NOI is $500,000 but the
  leases add up to $420,000 — **it catches it, quotes the exact page and line, and
  says: re-trade or kill.**
- It never averages a conflict. The lease outranks the estoppel outranks the rent roll
  outranks the T-12 outranks the flyer. Always.
- Builds the three NOIs: what the seller CLAIMS → what the documents PROVE → what a
  LENDER will accept.
- Stress test: what if rent drops 10%? Rates up? Tenant leaves? Exit cap worse?
  Shows you exactly where the deal breaks.
- **The tax trap:** when you buy, the county often raises the property tax to your
  price. It knows each state's rules (California Prop 13, Texas, Florida...) and shows
  the real NOI after the tax jump. This one catch pays for the whole machine.
- Reads the lease clauses too: rent bumps, renewal options, notice deadlines, CAM caps,
  exclusive-use rights, co-tenancy clauses, "can I even sublease this?"
- Checks the seller's Excel model for tricks: hidden rows, hidden sheets, hardcoded
  numbers pretending to be formulas.
- Tells you the MAX price to pay and still hit your target return, with today's
  real debt costs.

## STAGE 4 — THE CHERIF MOVE: CONTROL BEFORE YOU BUY

- Sees a vacant building → checks which national tenants fit that exact site
  (size, traffic, demographics, who's next door) from its tenant playbook.
- Does the math on "buy it empty, put a tenant in it": what it's worth vacant,
  what it's worth leased, minus TI, commissions, carry, downtime = your created value.
- Compares every control structure: option, master lease, seller financing,
  long close, assignment — with what each risks and what problem it solves
  for the seller.
- Writes the pitch to the owner AND the pitch to the tenant.
- **Master-lease arbitrage** (rent it, sublease it, keep the spread) — with the honest
  warning first: you owe the rent even if your subtenant leaves. And it checks the
  lease actually ALLOWS subleasing before you dream. Then it runs the whole operation
  after signing: rent in vs. rent owed, how many months of reserves left, what breaks
  the deal.

## STAGE 5 — MAKING THE OFFER & NEGOTIATING

- Recommends opening offer, target, and walk-away — with the reasons shown.
- Writes the LOI (for your attorney to finish).
- Builds a negotiation plan: what to anchor, what to trade, what each concession
  actually costs you vs. what it's worth to them (a $50k price cut vs. 30 extra
  days of DD are not the same thing — it prices both).
- When their counter comes in, it coaches: accept, counter, hold, or walk.
- **Catches contract drift:** you agreed on a $100k deposit and a Phase I contingency
  in the LOI — the PSA shows up with $150k and the Phase I quietly gone.
  It catches both and shows the exact language.
- Keeps a log of every verbal promise ("we'll fix the roof") so nothing gets forgotten.

## STAGE 6 — DUE DILIGENCE & CLOSING (where deals die)

- Builds the full checklist for YOUR deal type: leases, estoppels, title, survey,
  Phase I, inspection... with deadlines and who owes what.
- Data-room score: "you have 60% of the documents, here are the 12 missing, these 3
  block your loan."
- Title report: finds the lawsuit (deal-stopper), the unpaid mortgage with no payoff
  arranged, the easement running through your building pad (fatal).
- Inspection findings → a real budget: fix now / year 1 / year 5 / year 10.
- Building age tells it what to test: built 1975 = asbestos + lead paint check.
- Closing statement: recomputes every proration to the penny. One cent off = flagged.
- **Wire safety:** it will NEVER approve wire instructions by itself. Every wire
  demands a human phone call-back. Wire fraud is unrecoverable — hard rule.
- Every deadline that can kill the deal is on one screen, every morning.

## STAGE 8 — RAISING MONEY & SELLING

- Investor waterfall math (pref, splits, promote) — transparent.
- SEC guardrails built in: 506(b) vs 506(c) rules enforced, PPM and Form D drafts
  for the securities attorney. It blocks illegal solicitation moves BEFORE they happen.
- Investor reports where every number traces to a source.
- When selling: readiness audit 6-12 months early (estoppels? title clean? books
  clean?), buyer list, and bid comparison that sees through a fake-high offer —
  a $4.4M bid with a tiny deposit and 60 days of outs is worth LESS than a clean
  $4.25M cash bid with $250k hard. It shows that math.
- 1031 exchange: the 45/180-day clocks, ID rules, replacement hunting.

## WHAT IT WILL NEVER DO (and why you can trust it)

- **Never pretends.** Every number shows its source. Missing means missing —
  it never fills a blank with a guess.
- **Never plays lawyer or CPA.** It reads like one — finds the issue, quotes the
  clause, knows the state rules — but hands the JUDGMENT to your attorney and CPA
  with the file already prepared. You pay professionals for decisions, not for reading.
- **Never sends, signs, or wires anything.** It drafts. Humans approve.
- **Never hides a fatal flaw behind a good score.**
- It can't give you capital, credit, relationships, or reps. That's your job —
  and Dad's.
